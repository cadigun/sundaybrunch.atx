Thanks for visiting Pasture. This page outlines how Pasture (“Pasture,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, or otherwise interact with us online.

We collect the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and city or area. We may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies for these purposes.

We may use Google Analytics and other third-party providers for website hosting, analytics, communications, security, and other business operations. Advertising and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not knowingly use information you provide to us for targeted advertising across unrelated websites. If our practices change, we will update this Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with law. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Pasture is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of the website after an update means the updated Policy applies to your use of the website, to the extent permitted by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@pasture.place.
