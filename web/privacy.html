Privacy
policy.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pasture (“Pasture,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects and uses information when you visit our website or join our pre-launch list.

What we collect

When you join the pre-launch list, we collect the email address and city or area you provide. We may also receive basic technical information from the services that host and protect this website.

How we use it

We use this information to send Pasture launch updates, first-invite notifications, and location-related updates; understand where people are interested in Pasture; and maintain and improve the website.

Our form provider

Our waitlist form is powered by Tally. Information submitted through the form is processed by Tally on our behalf. Please review Tally’s privacy practices for more information about how it handles data.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers that help us operate the waitlist and website. We keep waitlist information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from launch emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us at hello@pasture.place. You may also ask us to correct or delete your waitlist information.

Updates

We may update this policy as Pasture develops. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@pasture.place.
